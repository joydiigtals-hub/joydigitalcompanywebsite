## The Hidden Costs of "Cheap" Rs 5,000 – Rs 10,000 Websites

Many Chennai entrepreneurs attempt to save initial capital by choosing low-cost freelance packages offering "Complete 5-Page Website for Rs 5,999". While tempting, these builds usually come with severe commercial trade-offs:

1. **Bloated CMS Themes with 30+ Plugins:** Budget vendors install off-the-shelf templates packed with redundant code. Within weeks, page load times slow to 6–10 seconds on mobile devices, causing over 50% of visitors to bounce before seeing your services.
2. **Security Vulnerabilities:** Outdated WordPress plugins are the #1 attack vector for malware injection and Google security blacklisting.
3. **No Lead Capture Architecture:** Budget sites often feature generic, broken contact forms with no spam filtering, no WhatsApp quick-connect routing, and no Google Analytics event tracking.
4. **Poor Technical SEO:** Generic builders lack clean semantic HTML heading hierarchies, dynamic schema JSON-LD, and mobile Core Web Vitals optimization, making it nearly impossible to compete on Google Page 1.

When you factor in ongoing maintenance, fixing security breaches, and lost customer sales, a "cheap" website often proves to be the most expensive mistake a growing business can make.
